# commonest bands that no reader would call an everyday word. An editorial
# override of SCOWL for the everyday-words view only; no checker reads this file,
# and `graded_words()` keeps every entry (ADR 0051). One word per line; a `#`
# starts a comment. Each entry was measured in band 10 and says why it is out.

cums      # vulgar, and in no sense an everyday form
hes       # `he's` with its apostrophe lost
leaved    # `left` is the past of `leave`; `leaved` lives on only in `four-leaved`
numbest   # superlative of `numb`, unattested in use
payed     # a misspelling of `paid` outside the nautical sense
re        # a fragment: the musical note, or `re:` without its colon
