I

Fünffacher Denckring der Teutschen Sprache

Five nested paper discs, each turning on its own. Read from the innermost outwards and the rings spell a German word: a prefix, opening letters, a vowel, closing letters, a suffix. The heading promises the whole of the language on a single leaf, and the instruction under it is to cut that leaf up.

Dieses Blätlein muß heraus geschnidten / in fünff Ringe zertheilet / und auf fünff gleich=grosse Scheiben von Papyr / also aufeinander gehefftet werden / daß man jeden Ring absonderlich umbdrehen kan.

This little leaf must be cut out, divided into five rings, and fastened onto five paper discs of equal size, so that each ring can be turned by itself.

Harsdörffer's instruction to the bookbinder, 1651

The device is a machine for enumerating candidates, and Harsdörffer was candid that enumerating is all it does. He recommends the rings chiefly for finding rhymes — hunt the rhyming syllable on the third and fourth rings, then turn the second ring against it, which is the sweep the scene's own rhyme panel performs. Whether a compound the wheel produces means anything, the user decides. The device does the enumerating; it does not do the judging, and that division of labour is the whole idea. It is the same one Llull's wheels make three centuries earlier.

✂

Notice where the Denckring begins. Harsdörffer cuts a page into parts in order to assemble words. In 1959, in a hotel on the rue Gît-le-Cœur, Brion Gysin sliced through a stack of newspapers while trimming a mount, saw the cut layers line up into sentences, and ran the same operation backwards — cutting finished language apart to see what it had been holding.

II

The Cut-Up

Two straight cuts divide a page into four; the quarters are rearranged, bottom-right to top-left, and you read across the join. That is the whole method, and it is the one the scene performs — two blades you position by hand, and a verdict on the text read back out of the pieces.

Gysin's claim for it was that writing was fifty years behind painting: collage had been ordinary in the studio since 1912 and was still unthinkable on the page. Burroughs took the technique into the Nova trilogy and pushed it further with the fold-in, laying a folded page against another so two texts meet along a vertical seam. Tzara had proposed something adjacent in 1920 — cut an article into single words, shake them in a bag, draw them out in order — but as a provocation rather than a working method.

What the cut does is break the sentence's forward pressure. Prose reads as a chain of commitments, each clause narrowing what the next one can be; slicing across the column snaps those commitments and leaves phrases adjacent that no author would have put together. Most results are rubbish. The value is in the small proportion that is not, and finding it costs exactly the labour the Denckring's user spends deciding whether a compound means anything.

What the checker can and cannot say

This is the one scene on the stage whose procedure is source-relative: a cut-up is not decidable from the text alone, because the question is whether every word on the table came off the page it was cut from. The verdict counts words across the join and names the pieces that are not words the source can account for. It says nothing whatever about whether the result is any good, and there is no version of it that could.

The page under the blades is {{ cut_up_words }} words over {{ cut_up_lines }} lines, written for the scene — a quotation would make the demonstration a rights question rather than a demonstration, and a public-domain page would drag a second century onto a stage that already has two on it.

Both machines make the same bet: that a mechanical operation on language can turn up something the operator could not have reached by intending it, and that the operator remains responsible for recognising which results are worth keeping. Harsdörffer turns rings and gets candidates. Gysin cuts pages and gets candidates. Neither device ever produced a judgement, and neither pretended to — which is also the reason both of them can be checked by a program at all.
