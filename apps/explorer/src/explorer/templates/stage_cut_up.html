{# The cut is committed by this button rather than by letting go of a blade. Two reasons, and the first is the act itself: you line the scissors up and *then* you cut, and a cut that fired on every release would make positioning impossible — the sheet would be in pieces before it was aimed. The second is that the two beats below only read as a cut if they start from a settled blade. #}
