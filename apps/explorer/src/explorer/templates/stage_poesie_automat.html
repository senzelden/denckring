{# The board: six rows of 71 character cells, and nothing else. Enzensberger specified an electromechanical split-flap display and described its letters arriving *Buchstabe für Buchstabe*, so a cell is the unit, not a word. 71 is derived rather than chosen: a line is six flaps plus the five spaces between them, and no flap is wider than eleven characters (the device files are written to that cap and the root suite holds them to it). Both cartridges therefore play on the same board. A module is not an element here — it is a *run of cells*, and the run moves when a neighbouring module turns to a longer or shorter flap. What a hand takes hold of is the transparent handle laid over that run, which the script keeps in place; see `.board-module` and `placeModules`. Not a `.scene-split`: 71 monospaced columns will not fit beside a panel, so the board takes the whole stage and the panel sits underneath. #}
