Anderer Laden draws another of this trade's shops and repaints it: the style is drawn separately from the sign, so it is paint and never content. Every sign is checked live — by paronomasia when a whole word was displaced, by portmanteau when one word was spliced inside another — and every shop sells what the word that landed belongs to. Haarmonie and Diminu'tif are the second kind, and the half the tradition is recognised by. The grammar is not checked: the rows decide that two texts stand in a relation of sound, and leave whether the result is good German to the writer.
