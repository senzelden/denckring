    65. Metrical licences, one switch each, opt-in¶

    Partly supersedes ADR 0014, and only when a switch is set. ADR 0014 checks strict stress and admits no substitution, and its Consequences call encoding scansion's licences a research problem that it would be overclaiming to say was solved. This decision admits four licences, each by name and each off by default, so ADR 0014 still describes every check made with the switches off. It does not claim to encode scansion: four licences are not all of them.

    Context¶

    The stress-scanning rows read each word as the pronouncing dictionary stresses it and ask whether some combination of its listed readings fits the line's pattern (ADR 0014, ADR 0054). Canonical English verse does not scan that way, and the gap is not the dictionary's fault. Poets take a small set of standard licences, and the checker admitted none of them.

    The public-domain sourcing pass for petrarchan_sonnet (ADR 0062) made this concrete. It screened 97 fourteen-line blocks from Milton's poems in Beeching's edition (Project Gutenberg #1745) and Wordsworth's Poems in Two Volumes (1807; #8774, #8824), and the row rejected every one. Two of Milton's sonnets came closest. "When I consider how my light is spent" failed on two words only. Line 10 opens "Either man's work", a reversed first foot, which is the commonest licence in English pentameter. Line 13 reads "Ocean without rest", where the dictionary has only 01. "Cyriack, whose Grandsire" failed on disapproves, which the dictionary gives as 001 and the line wants as 101, and on superfluous. Every one of these is a reading a prosodist would accept, and the row was left with no external case.

    The maintainer decided on 2026-10-14 to model these licences as an opt-in, off by default, with one switch per licence, a closed list for stress shift and a diaeresis rule for the syllable licence.

    Decision¶

    Four bool parameters, each off by default: initial_inversion, secondary_stress, stress_shift and diaeresis. They sit on LicenceParams, and every row that scans an iambic or trochaic line, which is every row that can raise wrong_stress except the five named below, takes them. Eleven rows take ScansionParams, which is MetreParams plus the four: ballade, blank_verse, curtal_sonnet, heroic_couplet, iambic_pentameter, ottava_rima, petrarchan_sonnet, rhyme_royal, shakespearean_sonnet, sonnet and spenserian_stanza. ballade, heroic_couplet, iambic_pentameter and petrarchan_sonnet took no MetreParams before, so they also gain feminine_ending. trochaic_tetrameter takes LicenceParams alone: its line already closes on an unstressed syllable, and one more would be a dactylic close, which is not what feminine_ending describes. alexandrine keeps plain MetreParams. It counts syllables and scans no stress, so three of the four switches would be parameters that do nothing there. The rows that scan dactylic or classical patterns (alcaic_stanza, sapphic_stanza, dactylic_hexameter, elegiac_couplet, double_dactyl) take none of these parameters and are unchanged. Their patterns are already sets of feet with substitutions or an anceps, and a reversed first foot or a promoted syllable is not the licence those forms take. tests/test_metre_licences.py finds the rows by behaviour: any row whose rules include wrong_stress must take the four, unless it is one of these five, listed there with its reason.

    What each switch admits:

    • initial_inversion. A line may also be read with the first two positions of its pattern swapped, when they differ: 01 read as 10 in an iambic line, 10 as 01 in a trochaic one. This applies to the first foot only, never to an anceps (?), and is added as an alternative pattern, the way with_feminine adds a feminine one.
    • secondary_stress. In a word of three or more syllables, an unstressed syllable (0) that is not next to a 1 may take a beat, so disapproves (001) may also be 101. Several such syllables may be promoted at once, provided no two of them are adjacent. Words of one or two syllables are unaffected.
    • stress_shift. The words on a closed list may be read 10 or 01 whatever the dictionary says. The list is prosody.SHIFTABLE: without, within, upon, into, unto, beyond, before, toward, towards, across, among, beneath, below, above, about, against, along, around, behind, between, despite, throughout, therein, thereon, wherein. The brief that proposed it also named whereon. CMUdict does not carry that word, and an unknown word already takes any stress, so it was dropped. A test holds that every word on the list is a disyllable in the dictionary.
    • diaeresis. A word may have one syllable more than the dictionary gives it. The new syllable is unstressed, and the vowel after it is free (?), as a monosyllable's is. Two rules decide where:
    • Phones. In the dictionary's first pronunciation, a W or Y counts when it follows a consonant other than K or G and precedes a vowel with stress 0 or 2. For Y, that vowel must not be UW or UH. The word's spelling must also contain an i or u before another vowel letter. Under this rule million, M IH1 L Y AH0 N, may be 10?.
    • Spelling. The rule applies to a word ending in -ious, -uous, -ial(s) or -ian(s) when none of its dictionary forms has as many syllables as the vowel-letter groups before the ending plus two. Each form that has one syllable fewer, and does not end on a stress, gains one. Under this rule ambrosial, 010, may be 010?.

    Scope. initial_inversion reads no dictionary, so it applies in every language, German included. The other three read CMUdict's stress or phones, or English spelling, and they are stated for English only. With any of them on, a German or French text is read exactly as with it off, and a test holds this for Goethe's Iphigenie. No rule has been stated for another language's pack. The three word-level switches apply only to words the dictionary carries. For secondary_stress and stress_shift that costs nothing, since an unknown word already takes any stress. For diaeresis it is a choice: both its rules start from the dictionary's forms, so a word the dictionary lacks keeps its heuristic syllable count, and a diaeresis in such a word is not found.

    A second pass, not a wider first one. line_metre scans every option with the dictionary's readings first, exactly as before. The licences are tried only when nothing fits. A licensed reading of a word is offered after the word's own readings. So a line that scans without a licence is never reported as needing one, and with every switch off the scan is byte for byte what it was. Each switch derives its readings from the dictionary's forms only, never from another switch's, so a switch admits its own licence and no combination of two. A test checks the full four-by-four table.

    Evidence names the licence. A word read through a licence shows it in its evidence value, 10 (stress_shift) or 10 (initial_inversion). A word licence tags the word whose reading it supplied. A line licence tags every word with a syllable on the reversed foot, and that does not mean the word's own reading changed: in "And disapproves that care", read on the inverted pattern, disapproves shows 001 (initial_inversion), its dictionary form, because its first syllable stands on the reversed foot. basis still says where the word's own readings came from (dictionary, ambiguous, estimated). Adding a fourth basis value would widen a closed set that every consumer of Evidence matches on, to say something the value can say for itself.

    Licensed forms count towards MAX_COMBINATIONS. The cap holds a line's combinations of readings to 4,096 and, past it, scans only each later word's first form. A licensed reading is one more combination, so in principle a line could be capped on the licensed pass where the dictionary pass was not, and a switch could then lose a reading another switch alone had found. It stays counted, because the cap exists to bound the work and the licences are where the work grows. Measured on the 10,611 lines of Paradise Lost below and on the same lines shuffled, no line is newly capped with all four on, and no line passes with one switch and fails with all four.

    No checker_version bump (ADR 0061). A parameter 0.5.0 would have rejected as unknown cannot move a verdict 0.5.0 gave, and the verdict fingerprints are unchanged on every existing probe.

    Consequences¶

    petrarchan_sonnet has its first external case: Milton's Sonnet XVI, checked with initial_inversion and stress_shift and passing under nothing less. It leaves the gap registry. The re-screen of the same 97 blocks, measured on 2026-10-14:

    setting blocks the checker admits
    default 0
    feminine_ending alone 0
    each of the four alone 0
    initial_inversion + stress_shift 1 ("When I consider")
    all four 1
    all four + feminine_ending 1

    The licences do not rescue "Cyriack, whose Grandsire". secondary_stress reads disapproves as Milton does. The line "That with superfluous burden loads the day" still fails, because CMUdict puts the primary stress of superfluous on its first syllable (S UW1 P ER0 F L W AH2 S, 10?) where Milton's line has it on the second. That is a dictionary stress, not a missing syllable. diaeresis gives the word a fourth syllable (100?), which makes the line eleven syllables and leaves its stress where it was. None of the four switches moves a primary stress, and that is deliberate.

    Every switch admits misfits, and the measured share is large. The test is Paradise Lost as Beeching prints it: 10,611 lines, extracted by script as the indented lines of four words or more, so a few editorial lines may be among them. Each was scanned as pentameter with or without a feminine ending. The same lines were then scanned with their words shuffled (seed 0). Each shuffled line keeps its syllables but is metrical only by accident.

    switch real lines that now scan shuffled lines that now scan
    none (baseline) 6,747 3,476
    initial_inversion +515 +615
    secondary_stress +942 +450
    stress_shift +69 +128
    diaeresis +10 +13
    all four +1,614 +1,286

    initial_inversion admits more shuffled lines than real ones. A reversed first foot is too common a shape to tell verse from a word salad. With all four on, the checker admits about four shuffled lines for every five real ones it gains. A caller who switches the licences on is asking a weaker question, and this table is how much weaker.

    The list is editorial. SHIFTABLE encodes a judgement about which words verse moves freely. Someone else's list would differ, and a word left off it fails where a reader would let it pass. The list is closed so that the judgement is visible and can be argued with, not so that it is right.

    The diaeresis rule has false positives, and the brief's simpler rule had many more. The sample was the 5,000 most frequent words that CMUdict carries. As first proposed, the phones rule (any W or Y after a consonant and before a 0 or 2 vowel) fires on 135 of them. Most are not diaereses: figure and regular (the y of a cu or gu), equal and language (the rounding of qu and gu), value and continue (the vowel u itself), forward and railway (a W no vowel letter spells). Applied in that order, the three exclusions above remove 53 (after K or G), 32 (Y before UW or UH) and 23 (no i or u before a vowel letter). The rule as shipped, phones and spelling together, fires on 56 of the 5,000. By the author's reading, 6 of those 56 are false: population, populations, reputation, ammunition, genuine and genuinely. The spelling check is made on the whole word, not at the glide, so in the first four a -tion elsewhere in the word lets a Y through. In genuine the dictionary's first reading already gives the u a syllable of its own after the Y. The other 50 are the classical cases, such as so-ci-al, re-li-gi-ous, mil-li-on and un-i-on. No reader would split all of them in one poem.

    The freed vowel is a choice, and it is measured. In the brief's version the new syllable was a 0 and every other mark stayed as the dictionary had it. Then the new syllable nearly always stands next to another 0, and no iambic or trochaic line fits: on Paradise Lost that reading rescued no line, and on the 1,358 lines of the petrarchan screen none either. With the vowel after the glide free, it rescues ten lines of Paradise Lost (celestial three times, Ambrosial, impetuous, Union, Communion, nuptial, Pernicious, Religious), each a diaeresis Milton's metre asks for. The cost is that the freed vowel may also take a beat, which is a small promotion folded into this licence. That is what Milton's Re-li-gi-ous does, and a caller who has diaeresis on has accepted it.

    The phones rule reads the first pronunciation only. phonemes is the pack's one view of a word's phones, so a diaeresis in a second listed pronunciation is not found.

    Don Juan I, re-measured with every switch (ottava_rima, the 222 stanzas of Canto I in Coleridge's edition, extracted as for the existing cases). By default the checker admits 3. With feminine_ending it admits 5. With all four licences it admits 5, and with all four and feminine_ending, 18. That still rejects 204: 113 on stress, 148 on rhyme and 115 on line length, and a stanza may fail on more than one. Rhyme is untouched by any of this, and it is now the larger obstacle. No further stanzas are landed. The licences were not written for Byron, and a Don Juan case passing only under all five switches would be evidence about the switches, not about ottava rima.

    White's ballades (1887), re-measured with every switch. The 77 poems the row's screen selects (stated in the tomson-ballade-of-asphodel case) give:

    setting admitted rejected of those, on stress
    default 1 76 49
    all four 1 76 30
    all four + feminine_ending 2 75 53
    metre 0101010101 0 77 31
    the same, all four + feminine_ending 0 77 31
    metre 010101 1 76 4
    the same, all four + feminine_ending 1 76 14

    The one ballade the switches add is "Ballade of Might-Be", with all four and a feminine ending. Nothing is landed from this: as with Byron, a case passing only under five switches would be evidence about the switches. The stress count can rise with the switches on, because feminine_ending admits lines one syllable longer than the metre that were refused on length and are now scanned for stress. The table shows it at the default 01010101 (30 to 53, on nine-syllable lines) and at 010101 (4 to 14, on seven-syllable lines). At pentameter, where such a line has eleven syllables, the count stays at 31.
