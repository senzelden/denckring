denckring · the stage

Three directions for every scene

Twenty-four working sketches — eight scenes, three ways each. Every one runs: turn the discs, slide N, cut the page, press the button. Pick one per scene and that is what gets built.

Each sketch is the real 1280×720 frame a scene is recorded in, with the real data behind it — Harsdörffer's 264 transcribed parts, the Automat's 360 flaps, Llull's nine letters at six levels, the fourteen strips of ten, and every neighbour the N+7 slider can reach, taken from the Open English WordNet list the scene itself walks. What is not real is the checking: a sketch has no check() behind it, and where a verdict is faked or a reading is canned, the note under that sketch says so. Nothing here is wired to the explorer; these exist to be chosen between, not shipped.
