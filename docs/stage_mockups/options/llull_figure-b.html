Ramon Llull · Ars generalis ultima, 1305–1308

Tabula · all 84 chambers

checked — a genuine chamber of 3 distinct principles

Every chamber of three distinct letters, all 84 of them, computed from the nine. Press one to read it; press a letter below to see only the chambers that contain it. The printed Tabula generalis has a count of its own that this project has not checked against a facsimile, so it is not given.
