Raymond Queneau · Gallimard, 1961

Cent mille milliards de poèmes

this poem, in fourteen digits

1

of 100,000,000,000,000

checked — every line is one of the ten these strips offer

The state of the book is a fourteen-digit numeral and the numeral is the poem's place in the total. Press a digit to step it; the line beside it changes and nothing else does.
