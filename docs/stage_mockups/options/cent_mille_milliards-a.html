Raymond Queneau · Gallimard, 1961

Cent mille milliards de poèmes

1

of 100,000,000,000,000

checked — every line is one of the ten these strips offer

Ten sonnets on card, cut into fourteen strips. Press any strip to lift it to the next of its ten. The machine is Queneau's; the strips are not — his are in copyright and these were written to his scheme, ABAB ABAB CCD EED.
